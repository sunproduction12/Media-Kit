Denver Hot Pot & BBQ

The table is the show. Now film it.

1What we found

All-you-can-eat hot pot and BBQ, cooked at the table

  • Guests in public reviews praise broths made from scratch and a clean, roomy dining room
  • Broths include tom yum, pho, seafood and vegetable
  • A reservation page and photo gallery on the website
2Why it matters

Hot pot is chosen by what people see

In Denver's "best hot pot" threads on Reddit, the top answers name other restaurants. The website doesn't link to an Instagram, Facebook or TikTok account, so the steam, the broths and the grill aren't being shown.

3What we would do

Show the steam and the sizzle

  • Short Reels of the broth pour, the bubbling pot and the BBQ grill
  • Group and date-night moments at the table
  • One reservation link in every bio and caption

Fresh Pho

Guests already love the broth. Show it.

1What we found

4.5 stars from 98 Google reviews

  • Reviewers praise the broth, fresh noodles, bánh xèo, cơm tấm and generous portions
  • Online ordering for pickup and delivery
  • A clean website with the menu and a Leave-a-review link
2Why it matters

Other people are already posting about you

A local Instagram post about Fresh Pho passed 670 likes, and guests talk about it in a Colorado food group on Facebook and on Reddit. Those posts send people to other people's pages. The website doesn't link to a Fresh Pho social account.

3What we would do

Own the conversation

  • Reels of the broth, the bánh xèo sizzle and the coffee drip
  • Work with local food posters, with clear disclosure and permission
  • One order link in every bio and caption
